# Voyage-Journal
A 2D nautical adventure. Sail across boundless oceans and log voyages in the Voyage Journal. Amid drifting journeys, encounter wonders and seek your inner compass, finding your true self amid the sea.

# How to Play Sailing Journal

## 1. Getting Started

**Windows Build (Zip)**

- You’ll receive a `.zip` archive (containing an `.exe` and a `_Data` folder).
- Extract everything into the same folder.
- Double-click the `.exe` to launch the game.
- If your antivirus pops up, click **Allow / Run anyway**. It’s a self-made portable indie game and is safe.

## 2. Goal

You are a small boat starting from the home waters. Your goal is to find three legendary islands (**Shoal Island / Starfall Isle / Silk Market Island**). Each island has story content and a shop. After visiting all three, return to the starting point to clear the game!

The three islands are randomly placed each run, so every playthrough has a new sea map. Rely on **Sea Route Intel** and **Lookout** to explore.

## 3. Controls

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| W A S D | Move the boat (up / down / left / right) |
| Mouse | Click UI buttons / advance story pages |
| Tab | Open / close the tutorial |
| During story | Click the screen to advance; press **Skip** to skip the story |

Reaching the edge of the map automatically switches screens (the camera moves to the next screen), so just keep sailing.

## 4. Four Things to Manage

You can see these in the corner of the screen: **Food / Fresh Water / Hull Durability / Gold**. Every day of sailing consumes food and water. Hitting reefs reduces hull durability. If durability reaches zero, your ship sinks = game over. So—don’t starve, don’t crash, and spend your gold wisely.

## 5. Four Skills

| Skill | How to Use |
|---|---|
| Lookout | Reveals nearby sea, letting you see reefs / wind / currents before moving. Very useful at the start. |
| Fishing | When food runs low, fish to replenish some. A reliable gain. |
| Full Speed Dash | Dash 2 tiles to travel faster, but reef collision damage is doubled. Use only in open water. |
| Drift | Let the wind carry the boat 1 random tile. A gamble when lost; costs no food or water. |

## 6. Four Sea Conditions

| Condition | Effect | Tip |
|---|---|---|
| Reef | Hitting it reduces durability (full-speed collisions hurt more). | Most dangerous—reveal with Lookout and sail around it. |
| Ocean Current | With the current: no food/water cost. Against it: double consumption. | Follow the arrows. |
| Wind | Tailwind moves you 2 tiles, headwind stops you completely, crosswind is normal. | Sail with the tailwind and avoid headwind. |
| Storm | Adds fresh water, but costs some hull durability. | When low on water, head into it to catch rain; only enter if your hull can take it. |

## 7. Island Shops

Each of the three islands has a shop. You can buy food/water, repair your ship, sell excess food/water for gold, and get unique upgrades:

- **Shoal Island:** Herbs (fresh water is free for the day)
- **Starfall Isle:** Astrolabe (larger Lookout range)
- **Silk Market Island:** Reinforced Sails (tailwind lets you travel farther)

🕵️ **Strongly recommended:** Buy **Sea Route Intel** (60 gold) whenever you see it. It directly tells you where an undiscovered island is, helping you avoid detours and clear the game faster.

## 8. Stuck?

- **Can’t find an island** → Buy Sea Route Intel or slowly sweep with Lookout.
- **Food/water running low** → Fish, or resupply at the nearest island.
- **Durability keeps dropping** → Avoid reefs and repair your ship at an island.
- **Not sure what to do next** → Press Tab for the tutorial, or check your objectives for hints.

Fair winds! Find all three islands and make it home safely! ⛵
